About PSquare

✦ ✦ ✦

PSquare Art Gallery is a digital museum platform combining heritage art with modern web technologies.

All artworks are stored in a local MySQL database and served dynamically via PHP and AJAX. Visitors can explore paintings, book tickets, submit reviews, and purchase fine art prints — all powered by a full PHP + MySQL backend running on XAMPP.
